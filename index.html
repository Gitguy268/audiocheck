audiocheck

Checks whether a music file is as good as it claims. Everything runs in this tab, and no file leaves your device.

Drop music files or a folder here

    What it looks for

    Each file gets a spectrogram and a plain verdict.

    • MP3 or AAC audio dressed up as FLAC or WAV
    • Hi-res files that are upsampled CD audio, or 16-bit audio padded to 24
    • Loudness, true peak and clipping
    • Files that are damaged or cut short
